## The 5-Stage Hydro Testing Procedure

Hydrostatic stretch testing ensures that the structural integrity of your high-pressure CNG cylinder remains resilient under normal filling and operating conditions.

### Stage 1: Degassing & Residual Gas Evacuation
Before removing the cylinder valve, all remaining natural gas is safely vented and purged with inert nitrogen in a controlled explosion-proof area.

### Stage 2: Internal Inspection & Endoscopy
The cylinder valve is unscrewed, and an optical inspection tool is inserted into the cylinder neck to inspect the inner wall surface for rust pitting, contamination, or crack propagation.

### Stage 3: Water Jacket Test at 300 Bar
The cylinder is enclosed inside a sealed water jacket chamber, completely filled with de-aerated water, and pressurized using hydraulic pumps to 300 bar. 
- The water displaced by cylinder expansion is precisely measured in a calibrated burette.
- When pressure is released, the residual permanent stretch is calculated.

### Stage 4: Internal Baking & Complete Dehydration
Post-testing, hot dry air is circulated through the inverted cylinder to vaporize every droplet of residual moisture, preventing internal corrosion.

### Stage 5: Valve Re-assembly & Leak Tightness Test
The valve threads are sealed using certified compound and torqued to specified manufacturer limits, followed by pneumatic nitrogen leak checks at 200 bar.
