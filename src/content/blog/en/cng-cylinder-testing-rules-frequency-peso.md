## Statutory Hydro Testing Requirements in India

Under the Gas Cylinders Rules and Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) regulations, every vehicle fitted with a Compressed Natural Gas (CNG) cylinder must undergo mandatory hydrostatic stretch testing periodically.

### How Often Must a CNG Cylinder Be Tested?

For commercial and private vehicles:
- **Standard Interval:** Every **3 years** from the date of the last test or installation.
- **Expiry Risk:** Operating a vehicle with an expired CNG cylinder certificate is illegal and poses severe safety hazards, including risk of catastrophic rupture during refueling.
- **Refueling Compliance:** Fuel stations are legally instructed to refuse CNG refueling to any vehicle lacking a valid, verified hydro-test compliance plate/certificate.

### Critical Safety Checks Conducted
During certified hydrostatic testing, the cylinder undergoes:
1. **Visual & Ultrasonic De-valving Inspection:** Checking internal corrosion, pitting, thread damage, and wall thinning.
2. **Water Jacket Hydrostatic Stretch Test:** The cylinder is filled with water and pressurized to **300 bar** (1.5 times the operating pressure of 200 bar).
3. **Volumetric Expansion Measurement:** Permanent volumetric expansion must not exceed regulatory thresholds (typically 10% of total expansion).
4. **Tare Weight Measurement:** Checking metal loss against the manufacturer's original stamped tare weight.
5. **Certificate & Compliance Plate Issuance:** Stamping the test date and fitting the official compliance plate on the vehicle.
