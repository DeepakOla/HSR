# SETUP_REQUIRED checklist

Production is not ready until owner configures and verifies:

- Business legal name and Hindi/English public names
- City, address, map pin, contact phone, WhatsApp number, email
- Working days, holidays, operating hours, slot capacities, daily cap
- Verified station-recognition credentials and proof
- UPI VPA/payee name
- Staff identities and roles via Cloudflare Access
- Evidence retention policy
